## Summary

Beginning January 1, 2027, a developer within §6-1-1702’s covered-use scope must make documentation available to each applicable deployer in a reasonably understandable form, protecting trade secrets and information legally protected from disclosure.

## What Counts

- Describe intended uses, known harmful or inappropriate uses, known limitations and training-data categories to the extent known.
- Supply appropriate-use and monitoring instructions, and meaningful-human-review instructions where applicable.
- Supply information reasonably necessary for §6-1-1704 compliance, notifying the deployer if information is withheld. Give notice of material updates and specified changes within a reasonable time.

## What Does Not Count

- Treating the duties as covering every use of every model regardless of §1702(3) and (5).
- Relying solely on public release notes without giving direct notice to each deployer as required by §1702(2)(b).
