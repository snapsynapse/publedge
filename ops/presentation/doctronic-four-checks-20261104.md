# Does an initial term tell us the pilot's current status?

A Utah source-checking exercise, prepared November 4, 2026. Historical teaching material, not legal advice or a finding about current permission to operate.

## The question

Can we conclude from the retained agreement and PubLedge record alone that Doctronic's prescription-renewal pilot is authorized to continue after October 24, 2026?

The bounded answer is no: these materials establish an initial participation period and an extension process. They do not establish whether a later extension was requested, granted or denied. That missing evidence is the next research step.

## Open it

Open the [retained agreement PDF](../../data/admission/sources/doctronic/2026-10-15/Doctronic-Final-Agreement.pdf#page=6), not an AI-generated citation. It names Utah's Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy, Doctronic and the Division of Professional Licensing. The body heading says "DRAFT AGREEMENT"; the same retained PDF includes signatures on PDF page 11. Inspect the document, not just its filename.

The [stored PubLedge demonstration remap](../../data/examples/instruments/us-ut-oaip-rma-2025-002.md) is `us-ut-oaip-rma-2025-002`. It is an editorial representation, not an authority-issued PubLedge instrument. The official document controls.

## Read it

Section 2, PDF page 6, states:

> The period of this Agreement is for 12 months, beginning on the date this agreement is executed.

Section 13, PDF page 11, states:

> Participant may request a single extension of up to 12 months for regulatory mitigation no later than 30 days before the end of the initial 12-month period. The Office shall grant or deny an extension request before expiration of the initial demonstration period in accordance with Utah Code § 13-72-305.

The ability to request an extension is not evidence that one was granted. The reference to § 13-72-305 is reproduced from the retained agreement, not an independent finding about the current codification.

## Own it

Would we sign a statement that the pilot is currently authorized, or that it has legally terminated, using only this packet? Neither conclusion is established here.

What we can say: "The retained agreement specifies a 12-month initial period and an extension process. We still need the later decision and its scope before relying on a current-status claim."

Next step: locate and read any later extension or amendment decision, identify its date and scope, and reconcile it with the original agreement and any agency status listing. If we cannot obtain it, record the gap instead of supplying a confident answer.
