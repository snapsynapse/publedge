Our Mission
The Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy is dedicated to regulating the bleeding edge of AI. We consult with businesses, academic institutions, and other stakeholders to facilitate dialogue on regulatory proposals, guided by three core objectives:
Observe and Learn
Protect the Public
Foster Innovation
Through our AI regulatory sandbox ( see Utah Code § 13-72-401 ) and our collaborative oversight process, Utah is setting a new standard for innovative governance and leading the nation in responsible AI policy.

Pro-Human AI Initiative
Utah believes AI should bring people and ideas together. That's why we've partnered with community leaders to create the Pro-Human AI Initiative . With a pro-human approach, AI can amplify human potential while remaining clear, accountable, and responsive to human needs.
The Office of Artificial Intelligence Policy (OAIP) partners with companies building responsible AI. We learn from real-world use and transform those insights into policies that support safe, effective innovation.
