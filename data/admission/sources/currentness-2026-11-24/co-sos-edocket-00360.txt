Description of Subjects/Issues
The Colorado Department of Law (the Department) is considering rules governing the implementation of SB26-189, the Automated Decision Making Technology Act (ADMT Act), and HB26-1263, the Chatbot Safety Act, (together "the Acts"). The specific purpose of this rulemaking is to create rules governing the ways in which the Acts shall be carried out including the clarification of obligations imposed on developers and deployers of AI technology, disclosure and reporting requirements, and consumer rights, and to clarify the obligations of operators of Conversational Artificial Intelligence Services to protect users and report annually to the Attorney General.
Purpose/Objective of Rule
These purpose of the proposed Chatbot Safety Act and Automated Decision-Making Technology Act Rules is to implement and clarify both Acts by establishing standards and processes and ensuring that requirements are understandable and effective. The Automated Decision-Making Technology Act requires the Colorado Attorney General to adopt rules to clarify and implement the post-Adverse Outcome disclosure and Meaningful Human Review requirements by January 1, 2027. C.R.S. §§ 6-1-17041705 (eff. Jan. 1, 2027)
