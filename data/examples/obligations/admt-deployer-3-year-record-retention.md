## Summary

A deployer must retain records reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance for at least three years after the consequential decision, or longer if applicable state or federal law requires. The Act applies to consequential decisions made on or after January 1, 2027.

## What Counts

- Where applicable, records may include ADMT version identifiers, changelogs and documentation of material mitigation changes.

## What Does Not Count

- Starting the deployer’s three-year period when a system record was created. Record creation starts the developer’s separate clock under §6-1-1702(4).

## Statute Anchors

- [Colorado SB 26-189 signed act, §6-1-1703](https://leg.colorado.gov/bill_files/116489/download)
